SILENT HILL / TWILIGHT PRINCESS - 0.7.0 OLD SILENT HILL

This is not the complete Silent Hill campaign (about 20% of the full game is built).

WHAT'S NEW
- The whole of Old Silent Hill: 115 original town tiles in 17 areas.
  Walk off the end of a street to move into the next area (short fade, like
  Hyrule Field). 12 areas connect to the starting street (about 51,000 m2).
  The west side (5 areas) lies across one of Silent Hill's collapsed-road
  chasms; as in the original game it is reached later through the story route,
  which is not built yet. Chasm edges have invisible walls.
- Cafe 5to2 door works from the starting street, as in 0.6.
- Enemies: Stalhounds rise from the ground (Silent Hill's Groaners) and Guays
  circle in the fog and dive at you (Air Screamers). 64 in the connected town.
  They respawn when you re-enter an area.
- Fixes from your 0.6 test:
    * Inventory: Link now starts with the Lantern (flashlight), Hero's Bow
      (handgun, 30 arrows) and two Red Potions (health drinks), plus the
      Ordon Sword, Hylian Shield and 5 hearts. The empty item screens came from
      the test starting without a normal save.
    * Jump: Twilight Princess's jump is tuned to always lunge forward at full
      speed. The jump button now has a fixed height (90 cm, adjustable): straight
      up when standing, keeps your speed when running.
    * Dodge roll: RB rolls in the direction of the left stick, even when standing
      still or locked on. A while running still does the normal roll.

CONTROLS (Xbox controller, Dusklight default layout)
  Move ................ left stick          Camera ...... right stick
  Jump ................ LB                  keyboard: Space
  Dodge roll .......... RB (any direction)  Roll ........ A while running
  Sword ............... X                   Shield ...... hold right trigger
  Lock on ............. left trigger
  Side hop / backflip . hold lock-on, A + left/right/back
  Jump attack ......... lock onto an enemy, A
  Lantern ............. B                   Bow ......... Y
  Items / potions ..... D-pad (item ring)
  Pause screen ........ Start   (Options = Dusklight display/graphics settings)
  Dusklight menu ...... View / Back   (Silent Hill tab = jump height, buttons)

UPDATE AND RUN
1. Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy the contents of
   SilentHill-TwilightPrincess-Test over your test folder (allow replacement).
2. Double-click Launch-Silent-Hill.cmd. It uses your existing settings profile.
3. If graphics initialization fails, use Launch-Silent-Hill-D3D11.cmd.

TEST THIS BUILD
- Walk the streets in every direction and cross into the neighbouring areas.
  Note any spot where you fall, get stuck, or bounce back and forth at an edge.
- Fight Stalhounds and Guays; try the dodge roll, side hops, backflips, the bow.
- Open the item ring (D-pad) and drink a potion; light the Lantern.
- Jump standing and running. Enter the cafe and come back out.
- Watch the FPS display in the busiest areas.
- If anything fails, close the game and run Open-Silent-Hill-Logs.cmd.
  Send the newest log plus what happened and where you were.
