SILENT HILL / TWILIGHT PRINCESS - 0.8.0 OLD SILENT HILL + INTERIORS

This is not the complete Silent Hill campaign (about 22% of the full game is built).

WHAT'S NEW
- Buildings: 10 interiors of Old Silent Hill behind 24 working doors, placed
  where Silent Hill's own doors are. Walk up to a door and press A to open it.
  The west side of town is now reached through buildings, as in the original
  game. All doors are unlocked for now (story keys are not built yet).
- Fixes from your 0.7 test:
    * Crash after playing a while ("Destination buffer too small"): exits
      between areas were read from the wrong table while Link was between
      areas. Every area now shares one exit table, and a crash guard writes a
      log line instead of closing the game.
    * Sword, shield and blocking did not work: the Silent Hill areas were
      flagged as an indoor "no fighting" area (inherited from the cafe
      template). They are now flagged as a field, like Hyrule Field.
    * Item ring could not select or equip: same cause, same fix.
- The map button is off in Silent Hill areas (there is no Zelda map for them).

CONTROLS (Xbox controller, Dusklight default layout)
  Move ................ left stick          Camera ...... right stick
  Jump ................ LB                  keyboard: Space
  Dodge roll .......... RB (any direction)  Roll ........ A while running
  Sword ............... X                   Shield ...... hold right trigger
  Lock on ............. left trigger        Open door ... A
  Side hop / backflip . hold lock-on, A + left/right/back
  Lantern ............. B                   Bow ......... Y
  Items / potions ..... D-pad (item ring)
  Pause screen ........ Start   (Options = Dusklight display/graphics settings)

UPDATE AND RUN
1. Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy the contents of
   SilentHill-TwilightPrincess-Test over your test folder (allow replacement).
2. Double-click Launch-Silent-Hill.cmd.
3. If graphics initialization fails, use Launch-Silent-Hill-D3D11.cmd.

TEST THIS BUILD
- Swing the sword, block with the shield, and equip items from the item ring.
- Enter every building you can find and come back out. Note any door that
  does not open, drops you somewhere wrong, or leaves you stuck.
- Play for a long session crossing many areas (the old crash case).
- If anything fails, close the game and run Open-Silent-Hill-Logs.cmd.
  Send the newest log plus what happened and where you were.
  Lines starting with "crash guard" in the log are especially useful.

OTHER LAUNCHERS
